GGAAG Finance Notes (blog.ggaag.com) is a personal blog about managing money well with boring, verifiable methods.

## What you'll find here

Notes on personal finance for people who want the reasoning, not just the conclusion: cash flow tracking, emergency funds, index funds and dollar-cost averaging, asset allocation and rebalancing, and how to recognize investment scams. Whenever a claim involves a number, I try to show where the number comes from.

## Editorial stance

Three rules I hold myself to: (1) show the math or say it's an estimate; (2) state the conditions under which advice applies; (3) admit mistakes in public when I make them.

## Disclaimer

Everything on this site is a personal note for educational purposes only. It is **not financial advice**. Markets involve risk, including loss of principal. Do your own research and consult a licensed professional for your specific situation.
