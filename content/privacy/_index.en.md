This policy describes what data this website (blog.ggaag.com, "the site") collects, how it is used, and what choices you have. By using the site, you agree to this policy.

## Information the site collects directly

The site is a static blog. There are no accounts, no comment forms, and no newsletters, so the site does not ask for or store personal identity information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

## Google Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate usage — page views, referrers, and approximate geography. Google Analytics collects device and usage data via cookies and similar technologies. You can opt out using [Google's official opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) or your browser's cookie settings.

## Google AdSense

The site may display advertising served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalized advertising in [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/) and manage third-party cookie preferences at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, I will update this page and the date above.
